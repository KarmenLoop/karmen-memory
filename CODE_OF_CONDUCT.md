# Code of conduct

Treat people respectfully. Keep technical feedback specific, constructive, and relevant to the project. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Use synthetic examples in public discussions. Do not post private memories, credentials, or customer data.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately to `info@karmenloop.com`. Include only the information needed to understand the concern.
